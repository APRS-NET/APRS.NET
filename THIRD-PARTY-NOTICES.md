## aprs-deviceid

`AprsNet.Packets` identifies APRS devices with data adapted from the
[aprs-deviceid](https://github.com/aprsorg/aprs-deviceid) list, maintained by Heikki Hannikainen, OH7LZB, and
contributors. The list is licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/) licence (CC BY-SA 2.0),
and so is the device table generated from it, `src/AprsNet.Packets/AprsDevices.Generated.cs`. The pinned copy of the
list is `tools/deviceid/tocalls.yaml`; its contact addresses are not copied into the table.
